Fluttershy - Pink & Yellow (Valenced), version 1.0.0-beta3.
Theme colours adapted from the original Fluttershy theme by Fluttershy2008.
Wallpaper: user's supplied Fluttershy collage, bundled unchanged.

This update softens the existing wallpaper through the Themes API (opacity 0.65,
blur 2), increases surface dimming to 0.86, supplies the dark plum surface colour,
and adds modern text, channel and icon tokens. Input fields and popups are opaque.
The existing plugin, group and theme IDs are preserved. No extra companion is needed.
The screen-aligned cover renderer retains the source aspect ratio and may crop edges.
No global image, touch, navigation or React hooks are installed by this theme.

After updating, fully restart Discord. If background sliders were customised,
reset Background to the theme defaults to apply the softer appearance.

Validation covers registration, wallpaper byte identity, deterministic packaging,
catalogue hashes, preservation of previous releases and unrelated entries, and
the contrast of modern labels over the darkest/brightest sidebar surfaces.
No physical Android test was available.
