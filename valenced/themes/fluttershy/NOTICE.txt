Fluttershy - Pink & Yellow (Valenced), version 1.0.0-beta5.
Theme colours adapted from the original Fluttershy theme by Fluttershy2008.
Text and icon colours are inspired by Fluttershy2008's Rainbow Dash theme.
Wallpaper: user-supplied Fluttershy scrapbook collage, bundled unchanged.

The Themes API draws the collage at opacity 0.60 with blur 1 and surface
dimming 0.86. Its screen-aligned cover renderer retains the image aspect
ratio and may crop the edges. The wallpaper is available offline.
